Foxtrot is an in-development FPS with a custom game engine written in C++. It has a Vulkan backend, multithreaded asset loading and integrates with Jolt physics. Assets are streamed in the background, and the blockout is hot reloadable and generated on the fly from config files.

The renderer is super modular and is currently tiled Forward+. The renderer has dynamic shadows and SSAO.

As well, Foxtrot has an in-house scripting language (now has Strata scripting integrated!) and a SSE+AVX and Arm NEON optimized math library.
